 ============ Kubernates Task - Cretae Nginx Pod ==================

  apiVersion: v1
  kind: Pod


  metadata:
    name: pathnex-pod


  spec:
    containers:
     -name: web
      image: nginx:latest
      ports:
       -containerPort: 80
